# Checked elementary deformation/intersection models

All rings are over a field k of characteristic zero. These are direct algebra calculations; no existing source files were altered.

## Extended Rees algebra and flatness

For a finitely generated ideal I=(f_1,...,f_r) in A, define

R=A[t,I/t] subset A[t,t^{-1}].

The presentation map A[t,U_1,...,U_r] -> R has kernel

(t U_i-f_i for all i):t^infinity.

Indeed localization at t makes these equations exactly the kernel; contraction from that localization is saturation. Omitting saturation can introduce t-torsion and extra central components.

Write R=sum_{n in Z} I^n t^{-n}, with I^n=A for n<=0. Then R/(t)=sum_{n>=0} I^n/I^{n+1}. After inverting t, R[t^{-1}]=A[t,t^{-1}]. Every nonzero polynomial in k[t] acts injectively on the latter ring, even when A has nilpotents, and hence on R. A torsion-free module over the PID k[t] is flat. Thus DNC is flat over the parameter line. This argument proves base flatness; it does not make the total space smooth, or every special fibre reduced.

## Origin of the affine plane

A=k[x,y], I=(x,y): R=k[t,u,v], x=tu,y=tv. The central fibre is A^2_{u,v}, while t=a!=0 identifies with the original A^2 using u=x/a,v=y/a. This family is abstractly a product; its map to the original ambient scheme collapses the special normal directions onto the centre.

## Nonreduced Cartier centre

A=k[x], I=(x^2): R=k[t,x,u]/(tu-x^2). The special fibre is Spec k[x,u]/(x^2), a line bundle over the double point Z=Spec k[x]/(x^2). I/I^2=(x^2)/(x^4) is free rank one over O_Z. Thus this is a regular embedding despite a nonreduced centre. The generic point of the underlying special line has local ring k(u)[x]/x^2, of length two; its fundamental cycle is twice that line. The total family is singular at the origin, despite its flatness.

## Nonregular origin in a node

A=k[x,y]/(xy), I=(x,y): gr_I A=k[u,v]/(uv). The normal cone is two lines, each with multiplicity one. The normal space Spec Sym_k(I/I^2) is A^2 since dim_k I/I^2=2. The cone sits inside it by uv=0. This proves the normal space and normal cone need not agree. The immersion has codimension one but needs two minimal equations and is not regular.

## Optional thick-origin model and saturation

For I=(x^2,xy,y^2) in k[x,y], the kernel of the extended-Rees presentation is generated by

 tU-x^2, tV-xy, tW-y^2, xV-yU, xW-yV, UW-V^2.

Verified with lexicographic elimination of q from the naive equations plus qt-1. Thus

 gr_I A=k[x,y,U,V,W]/(x^2,xy,y^2,xV-yU,xW-yV,UW-V^2).

Its cone fibre over the reduced origin is Spec k[U,V,W]/(UW-V^2), not a rank-two vector space. Already the smaller ideal (x^2,xy) illustrates why saturation is needed: t(xV-yU)=0 in the naive presentation, but xV-yU itself must vanish in the actual subalgebra.

## Tangency under DNC

Let D=(y=0) in X=A^2 and V=(y-x^2). DNC_D X=Spec k[x,t,v], y=tv. The schematic closure of V times G_m is tv=x^2; this quotient has no t-torsion. Its central fibre is x^2=0 in the normal plane with coordinates x,v. It is the normal line over the length-two intersection W=Spec k[x]/x^2, and its fundamental cycle is twice the fibre over the origin. The refined zero-section Gysin class lies in CH_0(W) and is 2[0]. The ordinary Gysin class on D=A^1 is the pushforward of this class, hence zero in CH_0(A^1); this does not make the local intersection multiplicity zero.

Directly, length k[x,y]/(y,y-x^2)=2. Replacing V by (x=0) gives the normal fibre once and transverse multiplicity one.

For a projective example where ordinary Gysin retains the degree, take L=(Y=0) and the conic C=(YZ=X^2) in P^2. At p=[0:0:1] this is the same affine tangency; L^![C]=2[p] in CH_0(P^1).

## Full blow-up versus open DNC

For X=A^1_x, centre 0, form Bl_(x,t) A^2_{x,t}.

- x-chart: t=xv, central fibre xv=0, a union of two affine axes.
- t-chart: x=tu, central fibre t=0, an affine line with coordinate u.

The exceptional axes glue by u=1/v into P^1. The other axis is the strict transform of X times {0}, an affine line meeting that P^1 once. Thus the full central fibre is A^1 union P^1, not just the normal line. Removing the strict transform leaves exactly the t-chart, Spec k[t,u], the DNC. The exceptional P^1 is the projective completion of the affine normal line; its removed point is the infinity hyperplane.

## Flat and nonflat elementary families

- k[t,x]/(x^2-t) is free over k[t] with basis 1,x. Fibres are two reduced points for nonzero t and a double point for t=0.
- k[t,x]/(tx) has nonzero t-torsion x. Its generic fibre is a point and its central fibre an affine line, so it is not flat.
- xy=t identifies its total ring with k[x,y], with t acting as xy. It is torsion-free over k[t], hence flat, and smooth for t!=0.

Over D=k[epsilon]/epsilon^2, the family x^2=epsilon is free over D with basis 1,x and total ring k[x]/x^4. The trivial deformation of the double point has total ring k[x,epsilon]/(x^2,epsilon^2). Both are local Artinian rings, but their maximal ideals need one versus two generators, proving nonisomorphism even abstractly.

The two sections x=0 and x=epsilon in A^1_D are distinct as embedded deformations in the fixed ambient scheme, although both are abstractly Spec D. The double-point equations x^2 and x^2+epsilon x are likewise distinct embedded ideals, but x->x+epsilon/2 identifies their abstract families.

## Blow-up charts and self-intersection

Bl_0 A^2 has charts (x,a), y=ax and (b,y), x=by. On overlap b=1/a,y=ax. The exceptional curve E is P^1. Normal fibre coordinates transform as y=a x, exactly the transformation for the tautological line O(-1), so N_E=O(-1).

To state a global intersection number E^2=-1, use the proper surface Bl_p P^2, whose local charts are the same. Its normal degree is -1 and E^2=-[point] in CH_0 of that proper surface. In the nonproper Bl_0 A^2, CH_0 is zero (it is Tot O(-1) over P^1), so the pushed-forward class vanishes; there is no global degree map on all zero-cycles of that affine-base surface. The intrinsic degree of N_E remains -1.

A line L in P^2 has normal bundle O_L(1), hence self-intersection +1. These signs cannot be read off from the set-theoretic intersection L intersect L or E intersect E.

## Excess formula in the regular case

For a regular embedding Z->X of codimension c and a map V->X, suppose W=Z times_X V is regularly embedded in V with codimension c'. There is a normal-bundle injection N_{W/V}->N_{Z/X}|_W, with locally free cokernel E of rank c-c'. Then the refined class of [V] is c_top(E) cap [W]. For transverse intersection c'=c, E=0 and c_0=1. For self-intersection V=Z, c'=0 and E=N_{Z/X}, giving the self-intersection formula. The formula needs these hypotheses, or the more general normal-cone formulation.
